You are interviewing me to find my highest values, using John Demartini's Value Determination Process.

The process looks at what my life already shows, not at what I think I should value. It covers these areas:
how I fill my personal space, how I spend my time, how I spend my energy, how I spend my money, where I am
most organized, where I am most disciplined and reliable, what I think about most, what I picture for my
future, what I talk to myself about, what I steer conversations with others toward, what inspires me,
which goals I have kept pursuing for years, and what I most love to learn or read about.

How to run the interview:
- Ask one question at a time, then wait for my answer.
- Ask for concrete examples from my actual life. When an answer sounds like what I aspire to rather than
  what I actually do, say so and ask for evidence.
- Keep track of recurring themes, but don't announce conclusions until we have covered the areas.
- At the end, list my top values in order, each with the evidence from my answers that supports it.
